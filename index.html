WebGPU Compute Exploration

Fourteen interactive examples of GPU computing with WebGPU compute shaders written in WGSL, from basic parallel operations to real-time physics simulations and ray-marched fractals. Each interactive example is set up as you scroll to it and runs when you press Start.

Compute basics

1Vector Addition

Adds two vectors of one million numbers in parallel on the GPU, one invocation per element.

2Matrix Multiplication

Multiplies two 512×512 matrices with 2D workgroups, a fundamental operation in ML and graphics.

3Image Processing: Gaussian Blur

Applies a 25×25 Gaussian blur (σ = 4) to a sharp-edged test image with a compute shader.

Simulations & rendering

4Conway's Game of Life

A 200×150 cellular automaton; each generation is one compute dispatch.

10 fps
Patterns

Interaction: click a cell to toggle it.

5Molecular Dynamics

Atoms interacting through the Lennard-Jones potential in a periodic 3D box, all forces computed on the GPU.

1000 30

Camera: drag to rotate · wheel to zoom · right-drag to pan

6SPH Fluid

2D Smoothed Particle Hydrodynamics: a dam-break fluid with pressure, viscosity and gravity.

50 30

Add Water drops a block of 100 particles from the top (up to 4,000 in total).

7Ray Marching: 3D Fractals

Sphere tracing of distance-estimated fractals in a fragment shader, with lighting and ambient occlusion.

8 128

Camera: auto-rotates · drag to rotate · wheel to zoom. Renders only while on screen.

8Boids Flocking

Craig Reynolds' flocking rules — separation, alignment and cohesion — for up to 10,000 agents.

2000 50
Behaviors

Interaction: hold the left mouse button to attract boids, the right button to repel them.

9Diffusion-Limited Aggregation (3D)

Random walkers drift through a 64³ grid and stick to the growing structure, forming branching, coral-like growth.

10 50%
Seeds

Camera: drag to rotate · wheel to zoom

10Reaction-Diffusion (Gray-Scott)

Two chemicals diffuse and react on a 512×384 wrap-around grid, growing coral, spots, mazes and other Turing patterns.

0.0545 0.0620 12
Presets

Interaction: hold the left mouse button and drag to paint chemical V. A preset or Reset reseeds the grid.

11Wave Equation

Ripples spread, reflect and interfere on a 512×384 water surface, solved with finite differences on the 2D wave equation and lit from its surface normals.

0.50 0.004 4
Presets

Interaction: click to drop a ripple, hold to keep dropping them · shift-drag to draw walls. Reset clears the water and keeps the walls; a preset replaces both.

12Falling Sand

Sand, water, oil, fire, plants and acid on a 320×240 grid. Every step, one GPU thread per 2×2 block moves and reacts its four cells (the Margolus neighborhood), so grains never collide.

6 3
Material

Interaction: hold the left mouse button to paint the selected material, the right button to erase; painting also works while paused. Try fire on the oil, or acid on stone.

13Procedural Noise

A compute shader evaluates 3D Perlin or simplex noise as fractal Brownian motion, with optional ridges and domain warping, for every pixel; a render pass colors it as terrain, clouds, marble or grayscale.

2.5 7 2.00 0.50 0.4
Presets

Interaction: drag to pan · wheel to zoom. Animate moves through the third (time) dimension of the noise.
