Learning path

Two ordered routes through the twenty-five examples. GPU programming builds up the parallel patterns, from independent threads to reductions, scans, trees and real-time deadlines. Computational science follows the numerics, from grids and stability to particles, Monte Carlo and spectral methods. Each step says why it comes next and gives you one thing to try, with links to the example's Explore, Understand and Measure tabs.
