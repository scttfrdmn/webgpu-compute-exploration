Physical-GPU validation

One click runs the validation protocol: the three flagship investigations — matrix multiplication, N-body and parallel sorting — with fixed settings, one experiment at a time, on this device's GPU. Each run checks its results against CPU references and produces a result record; together they form a validation bundle you can download and add to the validation matrix. Nothing is sent anywhere.
