For teachers and parents

Guides for running one session with one learner, at the learner's level. Each guide says what to set up, what to say, what not to explain first, how to help when the learner is stuck, and how to end, in about 25 minutes. Each ends with an observation rubric: four things to watch for — forming a prediction, changing one thing at a time, reading the measurement correctly, explaining the conclusion in their own words — each at three levels with examples.

The site collects nothing: there is no account, no tracking and nothing is sent anywhere. Write your observations on paper or in your own notes. Sessions with minors need the usual consent from parents or the school. If you share what you saw, share it anonymised, for example with the learning session form.
